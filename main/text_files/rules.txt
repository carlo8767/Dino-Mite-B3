Rules for the Time-Rangers:
P1:   P2:
A     left arrow    – move left
D     right arrow   – move right
W     up arrow      – jump
S     down arrow    – duck
Escape – Pause game
1. If Dino gets hit by a hazard – Dino’s Game is Over!
2. When portal appears, you need to get there in 5 seconds!

Catch Power-Ups! 

PU - Reduce Time Dino needs to survive by 5 seconds.
PU - Reduce hazards speed by half.
PU - Gives Dino a shield against hazards for 5 seconds.
